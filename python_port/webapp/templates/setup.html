TurboRisk - Game Setup

How to Play:

  • Assignment Phase: Territories are automatically assigned to players.
  • Placement Phase: Place your initial armies on your territories.
  • Attack Phase: Attack adjacent enemy territories by rolling dice.
  • Movement Phase: Move armies between your territories at the end of your turn.

Objective: Conquer all territories to win the game!
